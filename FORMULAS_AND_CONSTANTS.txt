================================================================================
2. FORMULAS
================================================================================

--- 2.1 Euclidean Distance ---

Formula:
    distance = √[(x2 - x1)² + (y2 - y1)²]

Parameters:
    p1: Point 1 coordinates (x1, y1)
    p2: Point 2 coordinates (x2, y2)

Returns:
    Float: Euclidean distance between two points

Implementation:
    Uses scipy.spatial.distance.euclidean()


--- 2.2 Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) ---

Formula:
                |p2 - p6| + |p3 - p5|
    EAR = ─────────────────────────────
                  2 × |p1 - p4|

Where:
    - Numerator: Sum of vertical eye distances (eye height)
      • |p2 - p6|: Vertical distance 1
      • |p3 - p5|: Vertical distance 2
    - Denominator: 2 times horizontal eye distance (eye width)
      • |p1 - p4|: Horizontal distance

Interpretation:
    • When eye is open:   EAR ≈ 0.3
    • When eye is closed: EAR ≈ 0.1-0.15
    • EAR < 0.21: Eyes are closing (drowsiness indicator)

Parameters:
    eye: List of 6 (x, y) coordinates for eye landmarks
         [p1, p2, p3, p4, p5, p6]

Returns:
    Float: Eye aspect ratio

Implementation:
    A = euclidean_distance(eye[1], eye[5])
    B = euclidean_distance(eye[2], eye[4])
    C = euclidean_distance(eye[0], eye[3])
    EAR = (A + B) / (2.0 * C)

Note:
    - Final EAR is average of left and right eye EARs
    - Formula: EAR_final = (EAR_left + EAR_right) / 2.0


--- 2.3 Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR) ---

Formula:
              |upper - lower|
    MAR = ──────────────────────
              |left - right|

Where:
    - Numerator: Vertical distance between upper and lower mouth points
    - Denominator: Horizontal distance between left and right mouth points

Interpretation:
    • Higher MAR value = Mouth more open (yawning)
    • MAR > 0.6: Yawning detected (drowsiness indicator)

Parameters:
    landmarks: MediaPipe landmark object
    w, h: Frame width and height (for coordinate conversion)
    mouth_indices: List of 4 indices [upper, lower, left, right]

Returns:
    Float: Mouth aspect ratio

Coordinate Conversion:
    pixel_x = normalized_x × frame_width
    pixel_y = normalized_y × frame_height

Implementation:
    upper = (landmarks[mouth_indices[0]].x × w, landmarks[mouth_indices[0]].y × h)
    lower = (landmarks[mouth_indices[1]].x × w, landmarks[mouth_indices[1]].y × h)
    left  = (landmarks[mouth_indices[2]].x × w, landmarks[mouth_indices[2]].y × h)
    right = (landmarks[mouth_indices[3]].x × w, landmarks[mouth_indices[3]].y × h)
    MAR = euclidean_distance(upper, lower) / euclidean_distance(left, right)


--- 2.4 Head Pitch Angle ---

Formula:
    pitch = atan2(dy, dx) × (180 / π)

Where:
    dx = chin_x - nose_x
    dy = chin_y - nose_y

Interpretation:
    • Positive angle: Head tilted down (drowsy indicator)
    • Negative angle: Head tilted up
    • pitch > 20°: Head nodding down (drowsiness indicator)

Parameters:
    landmarks: MediaPipe landmark object
    w, h: Frame width and height (for coordinate conversion)
    nose_tip: Nose landmark index
    chin: Chin landmark index

Returns:
    Float: Pitch angle in degrees

Implementation:
    nose = (landmarks[nose_tip].x × w, landmarks[nose_tip].y × h)
    chin = (landmarks[chin].x × w, landmarks[chin].y × h)
    dx = chin[0] - nose[0]
    dy = chin[1] - nose[1]
    pitch = arctan2(dy, dx) × (180 / π)

Note:
    - atan2 gives angle from horizontal axis
    - Result is converted from radians to degrees


================================================================================
3. DROWSINESS SCORING SYSTEM
================================================================================

--- 3.1 Score Calculation ---

Scoring Rules:
    1. If EAR < EAR_THRESHOLD (0.21):     Add 2 points (eyes closing - strongest indicator)
    2. If MAR > MAR_THRESHOLD (0.6):      Add 1 point (yawning)
    3. If pitch > PITCH_THRESHOLD (20):   Add 1 point (head nodding)

Total Score Range: 0 to 4


--- 3.2 Risk Level Mapping ---

Score 0-1:
    Status: "Normal"
    Color: Green
    Interpretation: Alert and awake

Score 2:
    Status: "Moderate Risk"
    Color: Yellow
    Interpretation: Showing signs of drowsiness

Score 3-4:
    Status: "High Drowsiness Risk"
    Color: Red
    Interpretation: Dangerously drowsy, immediate attention required


--- 3.3 Score Calculation Formula ---

    score = 0
    
    if EAR < 0.21:
        score += 2
    
    if MAR > 0.6:
        score += 1
    
    if pitch > 20:
        score += 1
    
    # Final score: 0 ≤ score ≤ 4


================================================================================
4. MATHEMATICAL OPERATIONS SUMMARY
================================================================================

Distance Calculation:
    Euclidean Distance = √[(x2 - x1)² + (y2 - y1)²]

Aspect Ratios:
    EAR = (vertical_dist_1 + vertical_dist_2) / (2 × horizontal_dist)
    MAR = vertical_distance / horizontal_distance

Angle Calculation:
    pitch = arctan2(dy, dx) × (180 / π)

Coordinate Normalization:
    pixel_coordinate = normalized_coordinate × dimension_size
